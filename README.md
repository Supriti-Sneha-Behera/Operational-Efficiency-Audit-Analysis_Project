# Operational Efficiency Audit Analysis Project

When a customer applies for a loan and they want their money fast. If the bank takes too long to say "yes" or "no," the customer gets frustrated and goes to a competing bank....
My project acts like a business detective for the bank—looking at every single step of a loan application to see where files get stuck, how much money those delays cost the bank, and what it takes to approve loans 20% faster.
